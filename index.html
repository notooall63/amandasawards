Where we travel

Serving Central NJ and surrounding areas.

We’re based in New Brunswick (08901) and regularly serve the surrounding region. Longer‑distance events are welcome—we just follow a simple, fair travel policy.

Travel policy

Events farther from our home base are welcome—we simply ask for:

  • 2 weeks’ notice for any venue more than 2 hours away
  • A $99 travel coverage fee for each additional one‑way hour beyond the first 2 hours (or portion thereof, calculated using your preferred navigation app)

For larger events (50+ guests), we tailor pricing to match your needs. All DIY pricing on our Square booking page applies automatically to events with 50 guests or fewer.
