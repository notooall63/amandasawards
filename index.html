Where We Travel

Serving Central NJ and beyond

We’re based in Central New Jersey and regularly serve the surrounding region. Longer‑distance events are absolutely possible — we just follow a clear, simple travel policy to keep things fair for everyone.

Travel Policy

Events farther from our home base (ZIP 08901) are welcome — we simply ask for:

  • At least 2 weeks’ notice for any venue more than 2 hours away
  • A $99 travel coverage fee for each additional one‑way hour beyond the first 2 hours (or portion thereof, calculated using your preferred navigation app)

For larger events (50+ guests), we tailor pricing to match your needs, staffing, and setup. All DIY pricing on our Book Now page applies automatically to events with 50 guests or fewer.
