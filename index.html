FAQ

A few quick answers. If you don’t see your question here, reach out — we’re happy to talk through your event details.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in Central New Jersey and serve the surrounding region regularly. Events farther from our home base (ZIP 08901) are absolutely possible — we just ask for:

  • At least 2 weeks’ notice for any venue more than 2 hours of drive‑time away
  • A $99 travel coverage fee for each additional one‑way hour beyond the first 2 hours (or portion thereof, calculated using your preferred navigation app)

For larger events (50+ guests), we tailor pricing to match your needs, staffing, and setup. All current do‑it‑yourself pricing options on our Book Now page apply automatically to events with 50 guests or fewer.

Do you handle setup and cleanup?

Yes. From setup to cleanup and everything in between, our team handles the full service so you can focus on hosting and enjoying your guests.

Can you work with our theme or decor?

Our default aesthetic is neutral to blend anywhere, but we’re happy to incorporate your decor, colors, or signage. Just share details when you book.

What about ingredients and sustainability?

We use high‑quality ingredients, well‑maintained equipment, and ONLY sustainable materials wherever possible. We’re serious about safety, quality, and minimizing waste.
