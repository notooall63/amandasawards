Do Dessert Caterers Handle Setup and Cleanup?

Yes — at Amanda's Awards, setup to cleanup is the service, not an add-on. The site puts it plainly: "From setup to cleanup and everything in between, our team handles the full service so you can focus on hosting and enjoying your guests." That means logistics, serving, and breakdown, with a venue that looks as clean when the team leaves as it did when they arrived.

What that covers

What does "full service" actually mean here?

  • Setup. The team brings the treats and the setup to your event, and gets it ready to serve — bingsu, flavored shaved ice, freshly spun cotton candy, and deep-fried cookies made with real Oreos.
  • Serving. Chilled treats are built to order with custom toppings, and cotton candy is spun on-site, so guests are served fresh at the event rather than from a pre-made tray.
  • Breakdown and cleanup. The team handles the breakdown as well as the serving, so the host is not left packing up equipment or tidying a serving area at the end of the night.
  • A self-contained setup. The team can operate in lobbies, gyms, cafeterias, and outdoor spaces, which is part of why setup and cleanup stay simple: the service brings what it needs, rather than needing the venue to supply it.

Amanda's Awards describes itself as a full-service confection-sweets event caterer, and that wording matters. A dessert drop-off — a box left at the door — is a different service from this one. Here, the price covers the whole job, from the first setup to the last cleanup, not just the desserts themselves.

Planning

What the host still needs to do

Very little, by design. The host's job is the booking side: share your event location when you book so availability and any travel details can be confirmed, share your guest count — 50 or fewer uses DIY Square pricing automatically, 50+ gets tailored pricing — and share any theme or decor details, since the default aesthetic is neutral to blend anywhere but the team is happy to incorporate your decor, colors, or signage.

For venues more than 2 hours away, the site's travel rules still apply: 2 weeks' notice, plus a $99 travel coverage fee for each additional one-way hour beyond the first 2 hours. Amanda's Awards is fully licensed and insured, using high-quality ingredients, well-maintained equipment, and only sustainable materials wherever possible.

Common questions

Questions people ask

Do I have to clean up after the caterer leaves?

No, not under this service. The site says the venue looks as clean when the team leaves as it did when they arrived — breakdown and cleanup are part of the job, not left to the host.

Does setup and cleanup cost extra?

The site does not list setup and cleanup as a separate fee. It describes setup-to-cleanup as the service itself. The only separate fee named on the site is the travel coverage fee for venues more than 2 hours away.

Does this apply to school and corporate events too?

Yes. The same full-service pattern applies to employee appreciation days, school fairs, fundraisers, and community events, as well as backyard birthdays, milestone celebrations, family reunions, holiday parties, and block parties — and the self-contained setup is built for lobbies, gyms, and cafeterias as well as outdoor spaces.
