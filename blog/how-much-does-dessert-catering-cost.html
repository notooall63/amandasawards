How Much Does Dessert Catering Cost for a Party?

There is no single flat price for Amanda's Awards dessert catering — it depends on how many guests you have and how far the event is. Events of 50 guests or fewer use the DIY pricing on the Square booking page, which applies automatically. Events of 50 or more guests get pricing tailored to that event. Travel is the only separate fee on the site: events more than 2 hours away carry a $99 travel coverage fee for each additional one-way hour beyond the first 2 hours.

What you get

What is included in the service?

Amanda's Awards is a full-service confection-sweets event caterer, and the price covers the whole job, not just the desserts:

  • Bingsu and Korean-style ice cream — chilled treats built to order, with custom toppings.
  • Flavored shaved ice — a lighter option that works for warm-weather and indoor parties alike.
  • Freshly spun cotton candy — spun on-site, in classic pink and fun colors.
  • Deep-fried cookies made with real Oreos — genuine, homemade-battered, made only with Oreos.
  • Setup to cleanup. The team handles the logistics, the serving, and the breakdown, and leaves the venue as clean as it was when they arrived.
  • Fully licensed and insured, using high-quality ingredients, well-maintained equipment, and only sustainable materials wherever possible.

Planning

How far in advance should you book?

The site names one hard lead time: events more than 2 hours away need 2 weeks' notice. For anything closer, there is no stated minimum on the site — but sharing your event location when you book lets Amanda's Awards confirm availability and any travel details before you commit, which matters most for corporate events, school fairs, and fundraisers that need a firm date.

Common questions

Questions people ask

Do dessert caterers handle setup and cleanup?

Yes — at Amanda's Awards, setup to cleanup is the service. From setup to cleanup and everything in between, the team handles the full service so you can focus on hosting and enjoying your guests.

Can a dessert cart work indoors?

Yes. The team is self-contained and can operate in lobbies, gyms, cafeterias, and outdoor spaces, and bingsu and shaved ice are described as perfect for indoor parties as well as warm-weather events.

What kinds of events do you cater?

Corporate get-togethers, employee appreciation days, school fairs, fundraisers, and community events — plus backyard birthdays, milestone celebrations, family reunions, holiday parties, and block parties.

What areas do you serve?

Amanda's Awards primarily serves its local region and surrounding communities. Share your event location when you book and they will confirm availability and any travel details — including the 2-week notice and $99-per-extra-hour rule for venues more than 2 hours away.
