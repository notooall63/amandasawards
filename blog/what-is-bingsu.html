What Is Bingsu?

Bingsu is a Korean shaved-ice dessert, built to order and finished with toppings chosen by the guest. At Amanda's Awards, it is served as part of the chilled-treats menu, alongside flavored shaved ice — a cold, light dessert that works for warm-weather events and indoor parties alike.

The basics

What exactly is it?

In general, bingsu starts with ice shaved very fine, so it piles up soft rather than crunchy, and is then topped to taste. That is what sets it apart from a plain cup of crushed ice with syrup poured over it: the ice itself is the dessert, and the toppings finish it.

At Amanda's Awards, the site describes it in its own words as "chilled treats, built to order" — Korean-style bingsu, flavored shaved ice, and custom toppings. Guests are not handed a single fixed flavour; the treat is built for them, which is why the site says the toppings "keep guests coming back for 'just one more.'"

At your event

What is it like at a party?

Bingsu fits the same full-service pattern as the rest of Amanda's Awards' catering:

  • Built to order, on site. It is made for each guest, with custom toppings, rather than served pre-made.
  • Good indoors and outdoors. The site calls bingsu and shaved ice "perfect for warm-weather events and indoor parties alike," and the team is self-contained, able to operate in lobbies, gyms, cafeterias, and outdoor spaces.
  • Part of a wider menu. It can sit alongside flavored shaved ice, freshly spun cotton candy, and deep-fried cookies made with real Oreos, so guests who do not want a cold dessert still have options.
  • Setup to cleanup. The team handles the logistics, the serving, and the breakdown, and leaves the venue as clean as it was when they arrived. Amanda's Awards is fully licensed and insured.

Common questions

Questions people ask

Is bingsu just shaved ice?

Not quite. Shaved ice is the wider category, and bingsu is the Korean version of it. A fuller comparison of Korean shaved ice vs. regular shaved ice gets its own post in this series — in short, bingsu is built to order with custom toppings, rather than being a single syrup flavour over ice.

Is bingsu good for an indoor party?

Yes. Amanda's Awards describes bingsu and shaved ice as perfect for indoor parties as well as warm-weather events, and the team is self-contained, so it can operate in lobbies, gyms, and cafeterias, not just outdoors.

Can you have bingsu and other treats at the same event?

Yes. Amanda's Awards brings bingsu, flavored shaved ice, freshly spun cotton candy, and deep-fried cookies made with real Oreos to events, so bingsu can be one part of the menu rather than the only option.
