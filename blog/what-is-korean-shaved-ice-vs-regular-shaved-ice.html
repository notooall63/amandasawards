What Is Korean Shaved Ice vs. Regular Shaved Ice?

The difference is mainly in the ice and how the dessert is built. Korean shaved ice — bingsu — is ice shaved very fine, so it piles up soft, and is built to order with custom toppings. Regular shaved ice is usually coarser ice with flavour poured over it. Both are cold, light desserts, but they do not eat the same way — and Amanda's Awards serves both, as chilled treats built to order.

Questions people ask

Is bingsu just a fancy name for shaved ice?

No. Bingsu is the Korean version of shaved ice, and the difference is in the fine shave and the built-to-order toppings. Shaved ice is the wider category; bingsu is one style within it.

Is one better than the other?

Not in any general sense — it comes down to texture and how you like a dessert finished. Fine, soft, topped ice is bingsu's style; coarser ice with flavour over it is regular shaved ice's style. At an Amanda's Awards event, you do not have to choose for everyone: both are on the menu.

Can I get both at the same event?

Yes. Amanda's Awards brings bingsu, flavored shaved ice, freshly spun cotton candy, and deep-fried cookies made with real Oreos to events, so both ice styles can be served together, built to order for each guest.
