Do Confection and Dessert Makers Come to Me?

Yes — with a caterer like Amanda's Awards, the desserts come to you. Amanda's Awards is a full-service confection-sweets event caterer, not a shop you visit to pick up treats. The team brings bingsu, flavored shaved ice, freshly spun cotton candy, and deep-fried cookies made with real Oreos to your event, and serves them there, fresh, from setup to cleanup.

How it works

What does "come to me" actually look like?

  • The service travels to your venue. Amanda's Awards brings the treats and the setup to corporate get-togethers, school fairs, fundraisers, community events, backyard birthdays, milestone celebrations, family reunions, holiday parties, and block parties.
  • The setup is self-contained. The team can operate in lobbies, gyms, cafeterias, and outdoor spaces, bringing what it needs to serve rather than needing the venue to supply a kitchen or a serving setup.
  • Desserts are made at the event. Chilled treats are built to order with custom toppings, and cotton candy is spun on-site, so guests are served fresh at your event rather than from a pre-made delivery.
  • Setup to cleanup is included. The team handles the logistics, the serving, and the breakdown, and leaves the venue as clean as it was when they arrived, so the host does not have to manage a dessert station or clean up after one.

Planning

What to share when you book

Amanda's Awards primarily serves its local region and surrounding communities, and the site asks you to share your event location when you book so availability and any travel details can be confirmed. That is the step that confirms the team can come to you for your particular venue.

The same booking rules apply as for any event: 50 guests or fewer use DIY pricing on the Square booking page automatically, 50+ guests get tailored pricing, and venues more than 2 hours away need 2 weeks' notice, plus a $99 travel coverage fee for each additional one-way hour beyond the first 2 hours. Amanda's Awards is fully licensed and insured, using high-quality ingredients, well-maintained equipment, and only sustainable materials wherever possible.

No. This is event catering, not a pickup shop. The team brings the menu to your event and serves it there, fresh, from setup to cleanup.

That is covered. The self-contained setup works in lobbies, gyms, and cafeterias as well as outdoors, and bingsu and shaved ice are described as perfect for indoor parties as well as warm-weather events.
