How Far in Advance Should I Book a Dessert Caterer?

At Amanda's Awards, there is only one hard lead time on the site: venues more than 2 hours away need 2 weeks' notice. For anything closer, the site states no minimum at all — so the practical answer is to book as soon as your date is firm, and to share your event location when you book so availability and any travel details can be confirmed before you commit.

What decides it

What actually changes how early you should book?

Your situation What the site says
Venue more than 2 hours away 2 weeks' notice required, plus a $99 travel coverage fee for each additional one-way hour beyond the first 2 hours (or portion thereof, calculated using your preferred navigation app)
Venue within 2 hours No minimum lead time stated on the site
50 guests or fewer DIY pricing on the Square booking page applies automatically
50+ guests Pricing is tailored to match your needs — that conversation is part of booking, so allow time for it

That last point is the one people miss. A small backyard birthday can often be booked on the Square page in a few minutes. A larger event — a corporate get-together, an employee appreciation day, a school fair, or a fundraiser — needs a tailored quote and a firm date, and those are exactly the events where a date slipping matters most. Booking early does not change the site's rules, but it does give that conversation room to happen.

Planning

How to book without guessing

When you book, share these three things up front, because they are the three things Amanda's Awards needs to confirm:

  • Your event location — so availability and travel details, including the 2-week and $99-per-extra-hour rules for venues more than 2 hours away, can be confirmed.
  • Your guest count — 50 or fewer uses the DIY Square pricing automatically; 50+ gets tailored pricing.
  • Any theme or decor details — the default aesthetic is neutral to blend anywhere, but the team is happy to incorporate your decor, colors, or signage if you share them when you book.

Amanda's Awards is a full-service caterer — bingsu, flavored shaved ice, freshly spun cotton candy, and deep-fried cookies made with real Oreos, with setup-to-cleanup service, fully licensed and insured — so once the date, location, and guest count are confirmed, the serving, logistics, and breakdown are handled for you.

Common questions

Questions people ask

Do I need 2 weeks' notice for a local event?

No — on the site, the 2-week rule applies only to venues more than 2 hours away. For closer events, no minimum lead time is stated, but booking as soon as your date is firm is still the safest move, especially for events that need a firm date.

What if my venue is more than 2 hours away and my event is soon?

That is the one case with a hard rule: the site asks for 2 weeks' notice for venues more than 2 hours away, plus the $99 travel coverage fee for each additional one-way hour beyond the first 2 hours. Share your location when you book so the travel time can be worked out with your preferred navigation app.

Is it too late to book?

The site does not state a cutoff for closer events, so there is no number in this post that would rule a date out. The fastest way to find out is to check availability with your date, location, and guest count — that is what confirms the booking, not a general lead-time rule.
