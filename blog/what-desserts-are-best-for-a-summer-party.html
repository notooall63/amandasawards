What Desserts Are Best for a Summer Party?

For a summer party, cold and light wins: bingsu and flavored shaved ice, built to order with custom toppings. Amanda's Awards describes those chilled treats as "perfect for warm-weather events" — and they sit well alongside freshly spun cotton candy and deep-fried cookies made with real Oreos, so guests who want something cold, and guests who want something sweet and warm, both have an option.

The menu

What works in the heat, and why

  • Bingsu and Korean-style ice cream — chilled treats built to order, with custom toppings that keep guests coming back for "just one more." This is the centrepiece for a hot day: cold, light, and made fresh for each guest.
  • Flavored shaved ice — a lighter cold option in the same chilled-treats family, also described as perfect for warm-weather events.
  • Freshly spun cotton candy — spun on-site in classic pink and fun colors, photo-ready and easy to hand to kids and adults alike. It is not a cold dessert, but it is light, fast to serve, and made fresh at the event.
  • Deep-fried cookies made with real Oreos — genuine, homemade-battered, made only with Oreos. The warm, indulgent counterpoint to the cold menu, for guests who do not want ice.

The point for a summer party is not to pick one dessert and hope it suits everyone. It is to offer a cold option, a light option, and a warm option, all served fresh at the event, so a guest who is overheated, a guest who wants something sweet but not cold, and a child who just wants cotton candy are all covered.

Planning

What to think about for an outdoor summer event

Amanda's Awards is self-contained and can operate in lobbies, gyms, cafeterias, and outdoor spaces, and its service runs from setup to cleanup — the team handles the logistics, the serving, and the breakdown, and leaves the venue as clean as it was when they arrived. That matters in summer, when an outdoor setup needs to be tidy, safe, and easy to host, not a second job for the host.

Two practical points from the site are worth checking when you book: share your event location when you book so availability and any travel details can be confirmed, and remember that venues more than 2 hours away need 2 weeks' notice, plus a $99 travel coverage fee for each additional one-way hour beyond the first 2 hours. Amanda's Awards is fully licensed and insured, using high-quality ingredients, well-maintained equipment, and only sustainable materials wherever possible.

Common questions

Questions people ask

Is bingsu too cold or too heavy for a summer party?

No — that is exactly what it is built for. Amanda's Awards calls bingsu and shaved ice perfect for warm-weather events, and because each serving is built to order with custom toppings, guests get a light, cold treat rather than a heavy, pre-made dessert.

What if some guests don't want a cold dessert?

That is why the menu is wider than ice alone. Freshly spun cotton candy and deep-fried cookies made with real Oreos are served at the same event, so a guest who does not want something cold still has a fresh, made-at-the-event option.

Does a summer party have to be outdoors?

No. Amanda's Awards describes bingsu and shaved ice as perfect for warm-weather events and indoor parties alike, and the team can operate indoors in lobbies, gyms, and cafeterias as well as outdoors — useful if the heat, or a summer storm, pushes the party inside.
